      How it works
      
      
        
        
      1. Extract reads 19 terms (pay, bonus, start date, notice, non-compete, early-exit cost, reply-by date…) verbatim, with character offsets and a normalised date or amount. A term the letter does not state comes back empty and becomes a question, never a guess.
        2. 
        
      2. Decide answers nine clause checks as yes/no probabilities, plus the type of engagement and an overall risk score. OfferLens turns probabilities into policy: red flag, review, or clear.
        3. 
        
      3. Fill builds the action record in one call: the exact sentence behind each flagged clause, a recommended next step, a three-sentence summary, and a polite email to HR with one question per risk and missing term.
        4. 
        
      4. Verify splits the generated summary and email into claims and checks each against the letter. Contradicted or unsupported claims block the "ready to send" state until you fix them; edits are re-checked on demand.
        5. 
        
      5. Protect makes a shareable copy with personal data replaced, seeded with the names Extract found.
        6. 
      
      
      
      OfferLens is a reading aid, not legal advice. Probabilities are model judgements; read the quoted clause yourself before acting on it.
